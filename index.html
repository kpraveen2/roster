01 · DISCOVER

Find the agents nobody registered

Read-only connectors enumerate agents, bots and automations across the platforms your teams actually use, and tie each one to the person who built or credentialed it.

02 · ASSIGN

Every agent gets a manager

A named accountable owner, a signed authority limit for anything that moves money or commits the company, and the approvals your industry's rules require: HIPAA, Opinion 512, FINRA 3110, TCPA.

03 · OFFBOARD

Triggered by your HRIS, not by luck

When Rippling or Workday says someone is leaving, their manager gets a checklist: reassign, rebind to a service identity, or retire. Tokens rotate, the record is signed, the auditor is happy.
